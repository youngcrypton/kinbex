## 2. Core Economic Alignment

$KINBEX ties together all participants in the ecosystem:

* **Traders:** Gain tiered swap fee reductions and zero-spread Kin Pay cashouts.
* **Alpha Callers:** Stake tokens to unlock verified leaderboard rankings, badges, and premium subscription monetization.
* **Project Founders:** Stake tokens to host Live Audio Spaces, sponsor game tournaments, and promote verified announcements.
* **Gamers:** Access high-roller tournament brackets and earn boosted staking yields on tournament winnings.
